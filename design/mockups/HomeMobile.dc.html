Move your media to the cloud. Keep your workflow.

Plume offloads your Media Library to Amazon S3, Cloudflare R2 or any S3-compatible bucket, and rewrites every URL so pages load from storage or your CDN.

Set it up once. Plume handles every upload after that.

  1. 01

    Connect a bucket

    Pick a provider, paste your keys and test the connection from the setup wizard.

  2. 02

    Offload your library

    New uploads go to storage automatically. Existing files move in a bulk job you can pause and resume.

  3. 03

    Serve from the edge

    URLs in posts and metadata point to your bucket or CDN domain, and caches are purged for you.

Nothing behind a license key.

Bulk migration

Move thousands of existing files in the background. Pause, resume and retry failures.

Restore any time

Pull files back to your server and revert URLs, one attachment or the whole library.

Cache purge

Cloudflare, LiteSpeed, WP Fastest Cache and proxy caches are cleared after each change.
